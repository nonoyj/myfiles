Back when nga naga pangaluyag palang ko saimo, before pa sang nag decide ka nga sabton ko, I knew in the first place nga ang decision ko nga to enter your life is worth it. Even though may mga sacrifices kag risks gid para lang maging kita and both of us know kung ano man ina.

Eversince that day nga gin sabat mo ko and until now wala gid ko dyapon regrets because You have loved me in ways I didn't know I deserved. You've seen me at my lowest and stayed. You've cheered for me at my highest that I will forever cherish.

Every small moment — aton mga rides, the late-night video calls, the shared silences, the inside jokes only and mga moments man nga feel ko hindi ko na gid kayanon ilog sang nadula si Kaisa. You helped me cope and gave me such purpose to still happily live kag batunon ang departure ni Kaisa and We both understand — they've woven themselves into the most beautiful chapter of my life. I am so incredibly grateful that it's you that I have in those moments.

Two years might seem small to the world, pero para sa akon, these have been the two most meaningful years I've ever lived. I look at where we started and where we are now, and I fall even more in love with the journey we've built together.

Ode to us — to every laughter yet to come, every adventure not yet taken, and every afternoon rides still ahead — with you, forever and always. Happy 2nd Anniversary, Loveyy!
